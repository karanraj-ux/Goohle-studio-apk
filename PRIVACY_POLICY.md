# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-06-24

## 1. Information We Collect
Auto SMS Forwarder and Call Shield ("the App") requires certain permissions to function, including SMS, Call Logs, Phone State, and Contacts (for VIP lists). The App processes these locally on your device.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
Your data is used strictly to provide the forwarding, auto-reply, and call shielding features. The App does not send your data to any external servers or third-party tracking services. All processing (such as checking if a number is a VIP) happens locally on your device.

## 3. Telemetry and Analytics
This application is completely open-source and free of proprietary trackers. We do not collect telemetry, crash logs, or analytics.

## 4. Third-Party Access
We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. 

## 5. Your Consent
By using our application, you consent to our privacy policy.

## 6. Changes to our Privacy Policy
If we decide to change our privacy policy, we will post those changes on this page.

## 7. Contact Us
